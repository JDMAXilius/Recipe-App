# Menus
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/menus
## Summary
A menu reveals a list of commands, options or states on demand, saving space. Clear verb labels, logical grouping and sparing, consistent icons make menus fast to scan; iOS offers small, medium and large layouts.
## Best practices
- Label items with a clear verb or verb phrase (View, Close, Select).
- Use title-style capitalization.
- Drop articles (a, an, the) to save space.
- Dim unavailable items; keep the menu itself openable even if all items are unavailable.
- Add an ellipsis (…) when an item needs more input.
- Use standard icons for common actions (Share, Print, Search).
- Use icons sparingly; skip one if none clearly fits.
- Icons for all items in a group or none.
- List important/frequent items first.
- Group related items with separators; keep related commands together regardless of importance.
- Watch length; split long menus or use a submenu (dynamic content like History may be long).
- Use submenus sparingly, one level deep, about five items max; indicate with a chevron.
- Prefer a submenu over indenting items.
- Toggled items: a changing label (Show Map / Hide Map), add verbs if ambiguous, or checkmarks for active attributes.
- Offer a single item to clear multiple toggled attributes (e.g. Plain).
## iOS specifics
- Small layout: top row of 4 icon-only items, then a list.
- Medium layout: top row of 3 items with icon over short label, then a list.
- Large (default): everything in a list.
- Use medium for three frequent key actions (Notes: Scan, Lock, Pin); small only for tightly related actions (Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough).
## Specs & numbers
- Small layout: 4 items in the top row.
- Medium layout: 3 items in the top row.
- Submenus: one level; more than about five items suggests a new menu.
- Submenu worth it when a term repeats in more than two items.

## Do / Don't
- Do: "Share, Add to Plan, Edit…, Delete" with icons on all. / Don't: icons on two items and none on the rest.
- Do: "Sort By ▸ Date, Rating, Time". / Don't: three items "Sort by Date", "Sort by Rating", "Sort by Time".
- Do: medium layout with Scan / Pin / Share on top. / Don't: small icon-only row for unrelated actions.
- Do: "Hide Nutrition" label that flips to "Show Nutrition". / Don't: both "Show" and "Hide" always listed with no state.
- Do: "Rename…" with ellipsis opening a field. / Don't: "Rename the Recipe" with an article and no ellipsis.
