## Noom (noom) · id634598719 · ★4.70 (873,953 ratings) · subtitle "Healthy Habits, Meals & GLP-1s"

## Patterns across this group
- The strongest moments **reflect the user's own input back as a concrete artifact**: Noom's dated weight curve with "Wedding" on it, Cherrypick's matches from your swipes and a priced basket at your supermarket, BitePal's raccoon with the personality you picked, PappAI's plan with your nutritionist's name. Generic "your plan is ready" does not appear in any of them.
- **Two kinds of magic**: transformation of something the user already owns (Crouton: messy page → clean recipe, seconds, no account) and a reveal of something built for them (Noom, Cherrypick, PappAI). Otto has both ingredients (import + questionnaire plan) and currently leads with neither.
- **Time-to-value**: Crouton is seconds (no onboarding at all); BitePal puts its emotional reveal about a minute in, mid-onboarding; Cherrypick and Noom run long quizzes (13-22+ screens) but end on a payoff tied to every answer. Long questionnaires are tolerated when the result visibly uses the answers.
- **Paywall placement**: Noom puts the paywall immediately after the forecast with urgency (countdown "plan reserved"), and the most-helpful-list review shows that converts excitement into resentment for some. Cherrypick and PappAI put no paywall before the moment (auto trial / free core). BitePal reveals the pet first, paywall later, and × leads to a free tier. Every app shows the moment before asking for money.
- **Account placement**: account after the moment and framed as saving it (Cherrypick "matches are ready — create account", Skip) or not required at all (Crouton). Noom asks for the account at screen 3; PappAI needs it because the plan comes from someone else.
- **Screenshot 1 sells the feeling or the outcome, not the feature list**: "Meals and groceries, sorted in minutes" (outcome + time), "Food tracker pet" (the character), "Your plan is written by your nutritionist" (who it's for), "A safe place for your favourite recipes" (ownership), "uses psychology to... keep it off" (the why). Screenshots 2-4 then show the mechanism (scan, plan, basket, timers).
- **A mascot works when it has a moment**: BitePal's reveal + personality choice is what users talk about; PappAI's mascots are decoration only. Otto's otter is closer to PappAI today.
