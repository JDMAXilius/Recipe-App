# Magic-moment study: B2-utility

Apps: CARROT Weather, Gentler Streak, Bevel, Oura, Amy, Ellie, Mogul. Researched 2026-11-04.
Shared sources: iTunes lookup + most-helpful RSS reviews (raw dumps in `scratchpad/magic/b2/<key>.json`), the App Store page for each subtitle (`b2/<key>.html`), and screenshots 1–4 at 600px (`scratchpad/magic/shots/<key>/b1–b4.png`, all viewed). Our lane study is at `docs/flow-maps/lanes/<key>.json`. None of these apps appear in `research/02_REFERENCE_APP_FLOWS.md` and none has a `maps/<key>.html`.
Labels: **seen** (I viewed the image or screen) · **doc** (official text) · **report** (third party) · **inferred**.
Note: Amy, Ellie and Mogul are all made by **AloaLabs, LLC**, a one-developer studio (reviews name "Chris"). All three have small rating counts (37, 61 and 23), so treat their review evidence as thin.

## CARROT Weather (carrot) · id961390574 · ★4.70 (87,934 ratings) · subtitle "Local Forecasts & Live Maps"
- **Problem it solves (user's words):** "a weather app that'll tell you the weather" while being fun to open: "weather with attitude".
- **Magic moment:** the first forecast for *your* current location, delivered with a joke aimed at you ("Make the most of this nice weather that I generated for you. Or else.").
- **Path to it:** about 3 steps: splash/"Greetings, meatbag." → Continue → iOS location alert → Weather tab fills from your location [seen, lanes/carrot.json lanes 3, 12, 14]. No account at all [seen]. The Premium Club paywall ("Seven days free, then $4.99/month", "Not Now") is part of the onboarding flow and can be skipped. The free tier gets the moment [seen, lane 4]. Whether the paywall comes before or after the location ask was not confirmed. Under 30 seconds [inferred].
- **How it makes it personal:** your city and live temperature, plus a line that talks to you by name-calling ("meatbag"). You pick from five personalities, from "professional" to "overkill" [doc].
- **Store page pitch:** 1 = no caption headline. The app screen is the pitch: "San Francisco 72°" with the snark line and a cartoon landscape [seen]. 2 = Radar/Forecast globe map with a hurricane band [seen]. 3 = "Crazy. Powerful. Hilarious." with App of the Year, Apple Design Award and Editors' Choice laurels [seen]. 4 = "Widgets: Forecasts on Your Home Screen", including a widget that says "I brought out the sun so I can blind you with it" [seen].
- **Users' words:** they never expected a weather app to roast them. Some reviewers ignored the snark at first and stayed for the depth of the data. A recurring complaint is that most features sit behind the paywall, and one calls the free tier "dangerously useless" for safety alerts [RSS reviews].
- **Evidence:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id961390574 (doc); RSS mostHelpful (doc/user); shots/carrot b1–b4 (seen); lanes/carrot.json, Mobbin flow cdc0bf0c (seen via our study).
- **What Otto can take:** (1) Make the first screen the product plus the voice. CARROT's screenshot 1 is just the app speaking a personality line over real data. Otto's otter could say one line about *this week's* plan. (2) Ask for one input (location) and show the value immediately, with nothing typed.

## Gentler Streak (gentlerstreak) · id1576857102 · ★4.71 (8,834 ratings) · subtitle "Activity, Exercise and Health"
- **Problem it solves (user's words):** "find fitness that's sustainable" without being "pushed to burnout with arbitrary goals". They want to "move consistently, not constantly" [doc].
- **Magic moment:** after Health connects, the mascot judges your actual recent workouts on a gentle "activity path" and tells you rest is allowed ("Rest is part of the plan. See you tomorrow!", quoted by a reviewer).
- **Path to it:** "Hi, I'm Yorhart" intro → name → Health pre-prompt "Fill me in" / Connect to Health → HealthKit sheet → notification pre-prompt (Skip / Go for it) → Streak tab [seen, lanes 3, 12, 14]. That is about 5–6 screens with no account [seen]. A paywall exists ("Go Premium 50% OFF", ×) but its position in first-run was not confirmed. The core streak is free [doc]. If the user has watch history, the path chart fills from Health right away [inferred]. With no history it shows "Getting to Know You… ready 7 days after next workout" [seen], so the moment is delayed by a week. About 1 minute with history, otherwise 1–7 days [inferred].
- **How it makes it personal:** your name, your past workouts imported from Health, and readiness computed from your own baseline. The mascot's copy reacts to *your* day ("Great Effort Today… This workout was fantastic!").
- **Store page pitch:** 1 = "Track your health & fitness". It shows the mascot running, "Great Effort Today", the green activity-path chart, a "101 km Outdoor Cycle" row, and a BGR quote: "isn't about closing rings – it's about taking care of yourself" [seen]. 2 = "Monitor your wellness" (body-metric tiles, Apple Watch) [seen]. 3 = "Analyze your sleep" (Sleep Quality "Excellent" with a cheeky line, "hardly worth noting") [seen]. 4 = "Your Data Stays Yours: Processed on-device. No user accounts. No servers." [seen].
- **Users' words:** it helped someone with fibromyalgia "finally gain strength" without crash cycles. It doesn't give a red X for resting. Several call it the only tracker they kept for years. Negatives: it's "always overreaching" for some users, and steps don't count as activity [RSS reviews].
- **Evidence:** App Store page (doc); RSS (user); shots/gentlerstreak b1–b4 (seen); lanes/gentlerstreak.json (seen).
- **What Otto can take:** (1) Make the first verdict kind: Otto's first plan should say why it fits *you* ("light week, 3 quick dinners") in a mascot voice. (2) Put "no account" up front as a trust slide if Otto can defer sign-up.

## Bevel (bevel) · id6456176249 · ★4.85 (17,712 ratings) · subtitle "Exercise, Sleep & Nutrition"
- **Problem it solves (user's words):** a "Whoop" without another wearable, so users can understand what the Apple Watch already collects.
- **Magic moment:** the first Home screen after Health sync shows Strain, Recovery and Sleep rings computed from your own watch data, with "Good morning, <name>. You hit your sleep goal… HRV is up".
- **Path to it:** welcome → Continue with Apple (or email magic link) → Health pre-ask → HealthKit sheet → Home ("Data still catching up" while syncing) [seen, lanes 3, 12, 14]. That is about 4–5 steps, with the **account before** the value [seen]. The app is free with a Pro upsell card on Home *after* the value [seen, doc]. Minutes, depending on Health backfill [inferred].
- **How it makes it personal:** existing watch history becomes scores on day one, with a named morning summary. Bevel Intelligence greets you by name and suggests chips: "Create a training plan / Personalized for you", "Log food" [seen].
- **Store page pitch:** 1 = "Your Connected Health Coach". It shows Today with 40% Strain / 41% Recovery / 75% Sleep rings, "Good morning, John" and the recovery summary, plus an Apple Watch face [seen]. 2 = "Works with all your wearables" (Garmin, Oura, Apple Watch logos; New and Noteworthy laurel) [seen]. 3 = "Go deeper with Bevel Intelligence" ("Welcome back, John!" chat) [seen]. 4 = "Create Training Plans" (Tempo Run workout detail) [seen].
- **Users' words:** one reviewer "was already very impressed" after one day. Another says it "helped me realize I was absolutely not recovering". Others call it a "Whoop killer" that reads their Apple Watch, and "hit the ground running right from the start" [RSS reviews].
- **Evidence:** App Store page (doc); RSS (user); shots/bevel b1–b4 (seen); lanes/bevel.json (seen).
- **What Otto can take:** (1) Reuse data the user already has. Otto's equivalent is importing recipes they already saved (links, screenshots, photos) so the first screen is *their* food, not a stock plan. (2) A personal morning-summary card ("Good evening, Juan, tonight is the 20-minute pasta") as the first thing they see.

## Oura (oura) · id1043837948 · ★4.86 (320,992 ratings) · subtitle "Health & Fitness" (the listing shows this as its subtitle; it reads like a category placeholder)
- **Problem it solves (user's words):** "I've had issues sleeping my whole life". Users want to know how they actually slept and how ready they are.
- **Magic moment:** the first morning after wearing the ring, when Readiness and Sleep scores appear for the night you just had.
- **Path to it:** a ring purchase first, then sign-up by email code → **web checkout for the membership** (free first month, card or PayPal, billing address) → "Membership complete" → pair the ring → first night → first scores [seen, lanes 4, 12, 14]. Before that the app shows "No Readiness Score — yet", explaining it appears after the first sleep analysis [seen]. So the moment comes after the account and after payment details, roughly 8–24 hours after setup [inferred]. Full personalization needs a baseline of about 2 weeks [doc: ouraring.com/membership; report: mindbodygreen].
- **How it makes it personal:** your own night is turned into a score, with a coaching line ("Go get 'em! A great night's sleep can boost your readiness"). Insights use population averages at first and personalize over time [report].
- **Store page pitch:** 1 = "Your Personal Health Companion". Today shows Readiness 88 / Sleep 84 / Activity 91 / Cycle day 6, a big "88 READINESS – Go get 'em!" over a mountain photo, and Learn more [seen]. 2 = "Get in Tune with Your Body" (Vitals: Readiness Optimal, Symptom Radar "No signs", Body Clock "Aligned") [seen]. 3 = "Invest in Your Health Future" (My Health: "Rising heart health, with a dip in stress management", Dive in with Advisor) [seen]. 4 = "Accurate Sleep Monitoring" (hypnogram, REM/Light/Deep) [seen].
- **Users' words:** a skeptic with past disordered eating was "made a believer". Another calls it a "game-changer" after a lifetime of bad sleep. One user wakes up each day to "a sense of how I slept". Complaints are about accuracy, paying a membership on top of the hardware, and battery decline [RSS reviews].
- **Evidence:** App Store page (doc); RSS (user); shots/oura b1–b4 (seen); lanes/oura.json (seen); https://ouraring.com/membership (doc); https://www.mindbodygreen.com/articles/oura-ring-review (report); https://z3n-cab-workshop-6.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9650633684634 (report, unclear provenance).
- **What Otto can take:** (1) Use a single score and a single sentence as the hero. Oura sells one number plus one plain-language line, and Otto's plan could open with one line about the week ("5 dinners, 1 shop, ~35 min a night"). (2) Name the wait honestly, the way "No Readiness Score — yet" does. If Otto's value only lands at the first cooked dinner, say when it arrives.

## Amy (amy) · id6753904989 · ★3.97 (37 ratings) · subtitle "Food tracking like Notes"
Confirmed: **Amy Food Journal** by AloaLabs, LLC (Health & Fitness). It is an AI calorie journal, not a different "Amy".
- **Problem it solves (user's words):** "food journaling that's not a chore". Earlier attempts "ended up being more of an obstacle to my day".
- **Magic moment:** you type a messy sentence like "Shin ramen with two eggs", and a calorie number fades in beside it with sources and Amy's "thought process".
- **Path to it:** first-run screens are not observed and not on Mobbin [lane 1, 14 unverified]. The app is account-based [doc] and new accounts get a 3-day trial "with no credit card", so the moment comes **before any paywall**. The plans appear "the next time you log" after the trial ends [doc, lane 4]. There is no free tier [doc, lane 5]. Seconds after reaching the journal [inferred].
- **How it makes it personal:** your exact words (brands, "2/3 chicken bowl… from Chipotle") are kept verbatim as the log line, and the estimate explains itself ("Something off? Click to edit") [seen].
- **Store page pitch:** 1 = "Track calories like writing in Apple Notes." It shows a Today note with plain-text lines: "Owyn protein shake 180 cal", "2/3 chicken bowl…" reading "Thinking", "7 sources", and "Shin ramen with two eggs ✦ 620 cal" [seen]. 2 = "Amy ✨magically✨ calculates everything" (Nutrition Details 620 cal, 6 sources, thought process) [seen]. 3 = "see your goals at a glance" (calorie and macro bars) [seen]. 4 = "hit your goals. unlock badges." (8-day streak, cat badges) [seen].
- **Users' words:** reviewers say showing or describing meals as they go is what finally made journaling stick, and that it's "surprisingly accurate" and like typing in Apple Notes. One "didn't expect it to be this good". A complaint: no support response [RSS, 15 reviews].
- **Evidence:** App Store page (doc); RSS (user); shots/amy b1–b4 (seen); lanes/amy.json, which cites amyfoodjournal.com/help/subscription (doc).
- **What Otto can take:** (1) Make screenshot 1 a live transformation, with messy input on the left and a structured result appearing. For Otto that is a pasted TikTok/link turning into a clean recipe card, or "what's for dinner, 2 kids, 30 min" turning into a plan. (2) Show the AI working ("Thinking…" turning into a result, with sources). The visible step is part of the delight.

## Ellie (ellie) · id1602196200 · ★4.64 (61 ratings) · subtitle "Time Blocking & Brain Dump"
- **Problem it solves (user's words):** "dump all my tasks from my mind into a list that I can then visually sort into my free time".
- **Magic moment:** dragging a brain-dumped task into today's timeline, next to your real calendar events, and seeing the day fit.
- **Path to it:** account-based sign-in (email / Apple / Google) [doc, lane 12]. Setup then asks you to "plan tomorrow", clarified in v4.2.1 [doc, lane 14]. The first-run screens themselves were not observed. The free version includes unlimited tasks, and Pro ($9.99/mo, $99.99/yr, $299.99 lifetime, 7-day trial) is sold later [doc]. The magic comes before any paywall. About 1–3 minutes, longer if you connect a calendar [inferred].
- **How it makes it personal:** your own tasks plus your Apple or Google Calendar events on one timeline [doc].
- **Store page pitch:** 1 = "Clear your head with Ellie… A Better Planner." It is illustration-only (a person with a phone and check marks), with **no product UI** [seen]. 2 = "Brain dump everything" (task cards, a hamster mascot saying "Perfect! Let's schedule it!") [seen]. 3 = "Drag into your day" (a task mid-drag into the Tue Sep 1 timeline) [seen]. 4 = "Give every thought a place" (sidebar of lists) [seen].
- **Users' words:** "Finally finally finally" from someone who tried every planner app. Others say it's the only one that "actually stuck" and got them through grad school while working. They praise calendar sync with both Apple and Google, and a responsive solo dev. Negative: "Way too expensive" for one feature [RSS].
- **Evidence:** App Store page (doc); RSS (user); shots/ellie b1–b4 (seen); lanes/ellie.json (seen).
- **What Otto can take:** (1) Use "dump then place": let users dump meal ideas or links, and Otto drops them into the week. A visible drag into the week is a concrete magic gesture. (2) A caution: screenshot 1 with only an illustration (like Otto's current quiet pitch) sells a mood, not a moment. Ellie's pitch only gets concrete at screenshot 3.

## Mogul (mogul) · id1367307210 · ★3.83 (23 ratings) · subtitle "Personal CRM/Network Manager"
Confirmed: **Mogul - Thoughtful Networking** by AloaLabs, LLC (a personal CRM and memory tool).
- **Problem it solves (user's words):** remembering details about people. Users describe a "simple ability to record information about interactions".
- **Magic moment:** opening someone's card before you meet them and seeing what they told you last time. The listing calls this surprising people "with your photographic memory". The moment is **delayed**: it only pays off at the second encounter.
- **Path to it:** log in (a web account; E2E users set an encryption password) → People list → "Create Person" or import iOS contacts / CSV → log an interaction [doc, lanes 3, 12, 14]. Empty states were not observed. There is a "Free Forever" Basic plan, so no paywall gates the moment [doc, lane 4]. A first entry takes a few minutes, and the payoff can take days or weeks [inferred].
- **How it makes it personal:** your own notes about your own people, timestamped ("Met in person 3 days ago").
- **Store page pitch:** 1 = "Say hello (👋) to Mogul. Say hello to more thoughtful networking" over a coffee cup, with **no UI** [seen]. 2 = "Mogul helps you build relationships" (People list with last-contact dots) [seen]. 3 = "Surprise people with your photographic memory" (Matt Mullenweg card with bio and interaction notes) [seen]. 4 = "Keep a journal of your interactions" (April 2019 calendar of calls) [seen].
- **Users' words:** they love the simplicity and the beautiful UI. They ask for Address Book integration and complain about no data export or backup (older reviews) [RSS, 11 reviews].
- **Evidence:** App Store page (doc); RSS (user); shots/mogul b1–b4 (seen); lanes/mogul.json (seen).
- **What Otto can take:** (1) A cautionary tale. A moment that needs data entry before payoff, sold with a mood shot, gets 23 ratings. Otto's questionnaire risks the same "input now, value later" shape. (2) If some value has to be delayed (e.g. "Otto remembers what your family liked"), seed it on day one with imported data, the way contact import shortcuts Mogul's empty list.

## Patterns across this group
- **The strongest moments reflect data the user already has, with no typing.** CARROT uses location, and Bevel and Gentler Streak read existing Apple Health history. The first screen is already about *you*. The weakest (Mogul, Ellie's setup) need you to enter things first. For Otto, the nearest equivalent is the user's own saved recipes, links and screenshots, or household facts in one tap rather than 7 screens.
- **Time-to-value splits into instant and overnight.** CARROT and Amy take seconds. Bevel and Gentler take about a minute *if* history exists, otherwise a week. Oura takes one night. The overnight apps say so honestly ("No Readiness Score — yet", "Getting to Know You… ready in 7 days").
- **Personal reflection comes as one number plus one sentence in a voice.** Oura's "88 · Go get 'em!", Bevel's "Good morning, John… HRV is up", CARROT's snark line, Gentler's "Great Effort Today", Amy's "620 cal" plus its thought process. The data is never shown bare. A character or coach interprets it.
- **The paywall sits after the moment in every app here except Oura.** CARROT, Gentler, Bevel, Ellie and Mogul have free tiers, and Amy gives a 3-day no-card trial and asks at the next log. Only Oura (hardware plus membership) takes payment details before the first score, and its hardware purchase has already committed the user. Amy is the closest analog to Otto (subscription-only, no free tier). It solves this with a trial that needs no card and lands *before* the ask.
- **Screenshot 1 sells the moment as real UI with real-looking personal data** in CARROT, Gentler, Bevel, Oura and Amy (the top 5 by rating count). Ellie and Mogul lead with an illustration or mood photo and are the two smallest listings. Otto's "A quieter kind of cookbook" mood pitch matches the weaker pattern.
- **Mascot and voice amplify the moment rather than replace it.** CARROT's robot, Gentler's Yorhart, Amy's cat and Ellie's hamster all speak *over* real output. Personality is the frame and the data is the picture. The otter should comment on the user's plan, not on itself.
- **Visible AI work is part of the delight** (Amy's "Thinking…" turning into "620 cal" with "7 sources", Bevel's chips "Personalized for you"). For Otto's import, show the link turning into the recipe live.
