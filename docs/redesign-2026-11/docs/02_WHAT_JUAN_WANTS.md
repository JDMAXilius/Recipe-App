# What Juan wants, from his own feedback

## Must have
- **Sign-in row:** Apple, Google and Facebook as icon pills side by side in one row, not stacked in a column. This is the original lineup.
- **Modern, not simple.** The app must not look dead, plain or AI-generated.
- **Meal plan based on dates,** redesigned from scratch to look like the best planners.
- **Otter shown properly:** large and correctly sized, not tiny.
- **Paywall without an X,** with images at the right size.
- **Onboarding shown only once.**
- **Membership page only from Account/profile** for people who already paid.
- Follow Apple's guidelines and learn from the study apps.

- Painted onboarding scenes and category art
- Warm voice: "Pull up a stool", "Back to the kitchen?", "Dinner, done. Otto's proud of you."
- Paw save, calorie pill, macro dots, nutrition ring, shopping notepad
