# Brand and voice

From the original file's Brand & Voice page and the app's code. Exact values: `reference_code/tokens.ts`.

## Current look
- Terracotta `#C4562E`, cream `#FAF4EA`, deep cream `#F3E9DA`, ink `#2A211B`, soft ink `#6E6055`, gold `#E8B04B`
- Macro colors are fixed: protein `#3B82F6`, carbs `#F0A020`, fat `#8B5CF6`
- Lora for headlines, system font (SF Pro) for body

Warm cream, serif and terracotta is also a common AI-generated look. The painted otter and real food are what make it Otto's. If you evolve the palette, show Juan why.

## Otto's art rules
- Cutouts on cream, at least 48 pt, in native shape
- Never stretch, tint, recolor, outline, rotate or flip
- Never place Otto over food photography or dense content
- One Otto per screen
- Lives on: sign-up hero, greeting, empty states, errors, loading and cook finish
- Not on: routine toasts, dialogs or recipe steps, except the existing cooking action art

Juan's latest request to make Otto feel alive may require changing some rules, such as animation. Flag those changes.

## Voice
One narrator, third person and warm. Otto never says "I". Plain verbs, sentence case, at most two short sentences. Honesty beats charm.

Specimens to match:
- "Back to the kitchen?" / "Otto kept your place."
- "Pull up a stool."
- "Dinner, done. Otto's proud of you."
- "Nothing saved… yet. Tap the paw on any recipe and Otto will keep it here."
- "We dropped the pan. Try again in a bit."
- "Otto worked this out from the ingredients. An estimate."
- "Found something good? Otto will copy it down."
- "Open. No plans, no guilt."
