# Apple rules that designs must respect

Summarized from App Review Guidelines and subscription docs. Check the live pages for exact wording. Claude could not load the Human Interface Guidelines pages, so please read those directly.

## Paywall, 3.1.2(c)
**Otto 1.0.21 was rejected on this rule.** Apple said the billed amount must be the most clear and conspicuous pricing element, with free trial and per-month pricing subordinate in position and size.
- Show subscription name, length and what is included
- Billed amount is the most prominent price
- Trial length and the price billed after it
- Restore, Terms and Privacy links
- No fake countdowns, spin wheels or tricks (5.6)
- Sign out and Delete account stay reachable, because a non-member cannot reach Account

## Accounts
- In-app account deletion is required, 5.1.1(v)
- Offer a privacy-friendly login option alongside social login, 4.8. Otto offers Sign in with Apple
- Do not make Apple's button less prominent than the others

## AI and data
- Explicit permission before personal data goes to third-party AI, naming it, 5.1.2(i)
- Otto must not need notifications or tracking to work, 5.1.2
- Ask for permissions in context, with a clear benefit

## Health and nutrition, 1.4.1
Nutrition is labeled as an estimate. Do not make medical claims.

## Reviews, 5.6.1
Use only Apple's system rating prompt. No custom "rate us" screens.

## Store screenshots, 2.3
Show the app in use, with fictional account data.

## AI recipes, 1.2
AI-generated content may count as user-generated content, so reporting and contact options may be needed. Flag this if your design adds sharing or community features.
