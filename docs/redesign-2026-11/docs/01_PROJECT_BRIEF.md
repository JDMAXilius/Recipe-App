# Project brief

**Product:** Otto: Recipes & Meal Plans, live on the App Store.
**Store link:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/otto-recipes-meal-plans/id6792195637
**Platform:** iPhone first. Built with Expo / React Native, backed by Supabase. The AI features use OpenAI.
**Mascot:** Otto, a painted otter chef.
**Business model:** Otto Club subscription, monthly or yearly, with a free trial and a hard paywall. Prices in the mockups are samples.

## What the app does
- Import recipes from a link, video, pasted text or photo, or write your own
- Browse and search built-in recipes, and save them with the paw
- Ask Otto for a recipe by chat or voice
- See recipes with scalable servings and estimated nutrition
- Cook in a step-by-step mode with timers
- Plan meals by day and build a shopping list
- Share the list with a household, and keep a journal of cooked dishes

## The problem
Juan says the app feels dead, simple and like AI slop. It works, but it needs to feel alive and modern, like the best apps people use today. The meal plan is the main complaint: it is seven stacked cards, not built around dates.

## Goal
Redesign the whole experience: visuals, flow, interaction and functionality. Think from scratch about how each screen should work, while keeping what is already good. The website comes later, so focus on the app.

## History of this redesign
1. **Original app:** the current design, in the original Figma file.
2. **Redesign v1 by Claude:** new identity in basil green and butter yellow. Good ideas, but it changed too much.
3. **Redesign v2 by Claude:** original brand with v1's best ideas.
4. **Your version:** Juan likes parts of both v1 and v2. Use them as references to beat, not templates to copy.

## Known product bugs, separate from this work
Fixes are prepared in code. Your designs should simply show the correct behavior:
- Paid members must never land on the paywall after signing in.
- The paywall has no X for non-members.
- Otter art must keep its real shape and never be forced into a square.
