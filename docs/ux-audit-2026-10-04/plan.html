F. Found along the way

Small defects surfaced while capturing. Not part of the copy plan, but worth fixing in the same build.

Chat crashes when opened by link
Opening otto://create or otto://chats directly throws “Couldn't find the bottom tab bar height”. Only reachable by deep link today, but a notification or share link into chat would hit it.
RevenueCat “LogOut was called but the current user is anonymous”
On launch with nobody signed in, the app calls log-out anyway. Harmless, dev-only log, one-line fix.
“Couldn't save. Try again.” on the first save
Seen at 5:05 in your recording: the first tap on “Save to my cookbook” failed, the second worked. Needs a look before 1.0.20.
Account shows “Current plan: Free” while the Club card sells
Fixed in build 40 for the card text; the row itself goes with item 14.
