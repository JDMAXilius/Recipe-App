What to notice
    Tap a screen to trace its paths.

    The idea in one line

    Show the dinner before you ask for the account, and show the account before you ask for the money. Everything else is a consequence.

    Today: launch gate → sign up → three paintings → paywall → Discover. The person gives Otto an email and a card before Otto has shown them a single meal. The new order: launch → five food questions → taste swipe → their first week of dinners → “save your week” (Sign in with Apple first, Not now allowed) → Otto Club right after the reveal → Today, with tonight's dinner already on it.

    Seven rules the map obeys

    1 · Launch
    No gate screen

    The launch frame is the Welcome frame. Returning people skip it entirely and land where they left off (HIG Launching).

    2 · Onboarding
    Questions are the onboarding

    The three paintings stay as a swipeable intro with Skip. The real onboarding is five food questions and a swipe — it produces something.

    3 · Account
    After the result, as the payoff

    “Save your week.” Sign in with Apple first and largest (4.8), then Google and Facebook in one row, then email. Not now keeps the week on the phone.

    4 · Money
    Right after the reveal, once

    Billed amount leads (3.1.2(c)), trial timeline, Restore, Terms, Privacy. Never seen again by members. Non-members meet it only at Club features.

    5 · Permissions
    At the feature moment, primed

    Dinner reminder at the plan reveal, camera at the first photo, AI consent (naming OpenAI) right before the first Otto call. Every denial has a fallback.

    6 · Tabs
    Five words, no actions

    Today · Discover · Plan · Cookbook · You. Add and Ask Otto become toolbar actions, not a tab (HIG: tabs navigate, they don't act).

    7 · Returning
    State, not Discover

    Same tab, same recipe, same cook step. Lapsed members keep every recipe and plan; only Otto's work locks.

    Free Otto and Otto Club

    The paywall is dismissible, so what's behind “Not now” has to be a real app. The split follows one sentence: you can cook with Otto for free; you pay for Otto to do the work.

    Free Otto
    The kitchen tools

    Browse and search Discover · open any recipe, scale servings, see the nutrition estimate · paw-save · Cook mode with timers · plan by hand on the week · shopping list · journal · the first week Otto built during setup stays.

    Otto Club
    Otto does the work

    Otto plans my week · Ask Otto (chat and voice) · import from link, text and photo · household shared list · lock-screen timer and widgets. 7-day trial, yearly leads.

    If Juan keeps the hard paywall, the map still holds: delete the “Not now” arrow on the paywall and the Free Otto node; everything before and after stays identical. That is the one decision this map does not make for him — see Decisions.

    Decisions only Juan can make

    The map draws the recommended answer to each. Changing an answer changes a few arrows, not the structure.

    A · Business
    Hard paywall, or Free Otto behind “Not now”?

    Drawn: Free Otto. Apple's guidance and the HIG favour it, and the free tier is the honest answer to “what happens when a trial lapses?”. Alternative: keep the hard wall — delete one arrow and one node. RevenueCat's data says hard walls convert ~5× better per install; the trade is reviews, ratings and word of mouth.

    B · Account
    Account after the reveal, with Not now?

    Drawn: yes. Answers and the first week live on the phone until sign-in, then write to the account. Cost: a local store for prefs + week and a merge step. Alternative: require the account at “Save your week” (no Not now) — Apple allows it only if the app genuinely needs an account, which household sharing and sync arguably are.

    C · Home
    Today as the first tab?

    Drawn: yes. The plan reveal ends on tonight's dinner, so landing on Today keeps the story going; Discover is one tap away. Alternative: Discover first, Today as a card on it — weaker for returning people who already have a plan.

    D · Offer
    A one-time offer when the paywall is dismissed?

    Drawn: no. Headspace saves its discount for cancellation; Cal AI's 80%-off-after-close trains people to close. If wanted, the right place is the Membership page when someone cancels.

    E · Tabs
    Drop the center ＋ and the Create tab?

    Drawn: yes — five navigation tabs, Add and Ask Otto as toolbar actions. Cal AI and MyFitnessPal keep a center ＋ and it works for them; Apple's HIG says tabs shouldn't be actions. Following Apple here was the brief.

    F · Sign-in row
    Apple first, or three equal pills?

    Drawn: Sign in with Apple as the top full-width button, then Google and Facebook as pills in one row, then email. 4.8 only requires Apple to be no smaller; Juan asked for one row. Both are satisfied if Apple sits above the row at full width — flag if the lineup must be strictly three equal pills.
