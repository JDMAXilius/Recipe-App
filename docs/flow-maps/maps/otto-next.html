Otto — the flow it should have (current)

Hard paywall · 4 tabs · drawn the way the Yazio study map is drawn · 2026-11-04 · a proposal, not the shipped app

Flow map

The entire Otto app as one flow: welcome → three paintings → six food questions → taste swipe → Otto builds your first week → save your plan (Apple / Google / Facebook / email) → dinner reminders → Otto Club, no X → welcome to the Club → Home, Plan, Cookbook, Account. Returning people skip all of it: auto sign-in, then Home if Otto Club is active, the paywall if not. Other doors (a dinner notification or widget, a shared link, an offline launch) run the same check, then open their screen.

The shape in one line

Show the plan, take the account, then the paywall. Members never see the paywall again; everyone else can't get past it.

Splash→Welcome→Paintings→Questions→Taste→Building→Your first week→Save your plan→Reminders→Otto Club · no X→Welcome to the Club→Home

Returning: splash → auto sign-in → Home if Otto Club is active, the paywall if not. Sign in from Welcome ends the same way.

How the map is drawn

Same grammar as the Yazio map in the PlanFuel study file (page “13 — Yazio”, section “07 · Flow map”).

Cards
One card per screen

Same size everywhere: the screen on the left, its type, its name, one line on what it does, and whether it's new or exists today.

Columns
One column per stage

Read each column top to bottom, then jump to the next column. Short straight arrows inside a column, one arrow between columns.

Branches
A labelled dashed arrow

No decision boxes. “I have an account”, “forgot”, “cancel” are dashed arrows with the words on them.

Long jumps
Their own lane

Returning people run along the bottom lane; tabs reach their columns along the top lane. Nothing crosses a card.

Today and Discover become one Home

Recipe apps don't split “today” and “browse” into two tabs. Kitchen Stories' Home opens on Today's Recipe and What to cook tonight, then the feed. Wabi opens on This week's meals, then Suggested recipes. Centr's Home is the date strip with today's meals. Otto's Home follows that, in one scroll: Tonight → This week → Discover, with search and Ask Otto at the top.

The shopping list lives in Plan (Centr, BitePal and Recime put it on the plan). Add a recipe is the ＋ in Cookbook. Household, journal and settings live in Account.

Every state at launch, and where it goes

Who opens the appWhat they see
New person, fresh installWelcome → paintings → questions → taste → building → first week → save your plan → reminders → Otto Club → welcome to the Club → Home
Signed in, Otto Club activeSplash, then straight to Home, on the tab they left. Never the paywall.
Signed in, no Otto Club (never bought, or the trial ended)Splash, then the paywall, no X. Restore · Sign out · Delete account on it. Their recipes and plans stay saved.
Has an account, signed out (new phone, reinstall)Welcome → I have an account → Sign in → Home or the paywall, same as above. Onboarding is never shown again.
Club status still loadingThe splash stays up (5-second ceiling, as in 1.0.23). A member never sees a flash of the paywall.
Offline at launchThe last known Club answer is used; Home shows the cached plan. A fresh install offline gets Try again on the splash.
Dinner notification, widget, shared linkSame launch check first, then the exact screen. Signed out → Sign in, then back to it.
