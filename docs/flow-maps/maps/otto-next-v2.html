What to notice
    Tap a screen to trace its paths.

    The shape in one line

    Show the plan, take the account, then the paywall. Members never see the paywall again; everyone else can't get past it.

    Launch→Signed in?→Welcome→Paintings→Questions→Taste→Save your plan (account)→Building→Your first week→Otto Club · no X→Reminders→Home

    Returning: Launch → auto sign-in → Club active? → Home (yes) or the same paywall (no). Sign in from Welcome joins the same check.

    Whose order this is

    Today and Discover become one Home

    Recipe apps don't split "today" and "browse" into two tabs. Kitchen Stories' Home opens on Today's Recipe and What to cook tonight, then the feed. Wabi opens on This week's meals, then Suggested recipes. Centr's Home is the date strip with today's meals. Otto's Home follows that, in one scroll:

    Top
    Tonight

    Tonight's dinner from the plan, big, with Start cooking. Empty night: "Open. No plans, no guilt." with Plan tonight.

    Then
    This week

    A small date strip of the next days' meals. Tapping a day opens Plan.

    Then
    Discover

    Search with Ask Otto beside it, categories, Otto's picks, new recipes. This is today's Discover tab, moved down.

    Where the rest goes: the shopping list lives in Plan, as Centr, BitePal and Recime do ("Shopping list" button on the plan). Add a recipe is the ＋ in Cookbook's top bar. Ask Otto sits next to search on Home. Household, journal and settings live in Account.

    Apple checks for a hard paywall

    3.1.2(c)
    Billed amount leads

    $39.99/yr is the biggest price; per-month is the small line. Trial terms are stated plainly.

    3.1.1
    Restore on the paywall

    With Terms and Privacy links that work.

    5.1.1(v)
    A way out without paying

    The paywall has no X, so Sign out and Delete account must be on it. Today's paywall already has both; keep them.

    4.8
    Sign in with Apple

    As large as Google and Facebook in the one-row lineup, with no scrolling.

    5.1.2(i)
    AI consent

    The sheet names OpenAI and appears right before the first Ask Otto or import, not during onboarding.

    Every state at launch, and where it goes

    Who opens the appWhat they see
    New person, fresh installWelcome → paintings → questions → taste → Save your plan → first week → paywall → reminders → Home
    Signed in, Club activeSplash, then straight to Home, on the tab they left. Never the paywall.
    Signed in, no Club (never bought, or the trial ended)Splash, then the paywall, no X. Restore · Sign out · Delete account on it. Their recipes and plans stay saved for when they come back.
    Has an account, signed out (new phone, reinstall)Welcome → I have an account → Sign in → Club active? → Home or paywall. Onboarding is never shown again.
    Club status still loadingThe splash stays up (5-second ceiling, as in 1.0.23). Never a flash of the paywall for a member.
    Offline at launchThe last known Club answer is used; Home shows the cached plan. A fresh install offline gets a "Try again" on the splash.
    Dinner notification, widget, shared linkSame launch check first, then the exact screen (tonight's recipe, the shared list). Signed out → Sign in, then back to it.

    Inside the paywall

    OutcomeNext
    Starts the trialApple sheet → reminders primer (Not now allowed) → Home
    Cancels the Apple sheetBack on the paywall

    Ask to Buy pending"Waiting for approval" on the paywall; opens the app once approved
    Restore finds ClubHome
    Restore finds nothing"No Otto Club found for this Apple ID" on the paywall

    Asked at the moment, never up front

    AskWhen
    Dinner remindersOnce, right after the purchase (Tiimo's spot). Denied → the app works the same; switch it on later in Account → Notifications.
    AI consent (OpenAI)First Ask Otto or first import. Not now → that feature waits; everything else works.

    RatingOn the cook finish screen after the third dish, once.
